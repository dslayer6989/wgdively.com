This uses Have I Been Pwned's k-anonymity check. Your browser hashes the password with SHA-1 and sends only the first 5 characters of that hash. The full password and full hash never leave your device.

The estimate looks for common passwords, keyboard patterns (qwerty, 1q2w3e), repeats, sequences, years and "l33t" swaps. Then it works out the time to crack at 10 billion guesses a second, an offline attack with a modern graphics card. It's a guide, not a guarantee. A random password from a generator always beats a clever one you made up.
