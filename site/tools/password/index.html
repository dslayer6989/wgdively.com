Passwords are made with crypto.getRandomValues, your browser's secure random generator, without bias. They're never sent or stored. Over 80 bits is strong for most accounts, and over 100 bits is overkill (in a good way). Use a password manager like Bitwarden or KeePassXC so you never have to remember them.
